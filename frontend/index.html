How to read this dashboard
How to Read
Start at the KPI row, then scan the charts for the team or day that breaks the pattern.
Colour is consistent everywhere: green = reviewed, amber = waiting, red = behind target.
Each card has its own ? tooltip explaining what it measures and how to read it.
Everything respects the period and team filters at the top of the page.
Actions
A team below 50% → talk to the lead and find what is blocking them.
Reviews drifting past 4h → files are being forgotten, or reviewers are overloaded.
A queue above 50% → escalate to free it before it compounds.
